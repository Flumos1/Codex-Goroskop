## Advanced Explanation

The 4th house is the field of home, roots, family base, and inner foundation. The Moon brings emotional memory, need, receptivity, and containment into that field.

This combines the house-topic layer with the psychological Moon as emotional need and inner image.

Do not read this as a factual claim about the mother or a guaranteed family story. It describes a symbolic emotional foundation.

The constructive expression is emotional rootedness, care for private life, and conscious relationship to ancestral or family patterns.
