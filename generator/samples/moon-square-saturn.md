# Sample Report: Moon square Saturn

- System: western
- Method family: psychological-archetypal
- Confidence: medium
- Source rule: rule.western.aspect-to-inner-statement
- Source IDs: source.greene-sasportas-development-personality-v1

## Simple Explanation

A part of you may be careful with need, tenderness, and emotional dependency.

You may expect warmth to come with distance, effort, conditions, or responsibility.

The growth path is to build trust slowly and give your emotional life structure, patience, and protection.

Reflection: Where do you still treat your own needs as something that must be earned?

## Advanced Explanation

Moon square Saturn is interpreted as tension between the lunar principle of need, memory, and emotional containment and the Saturnian principle of limit, restraint, maturity, and effort.

The psychological-archetypal method renders the aspect as an inner expectation or life statement, not as a guaranteed event.

This should not be read as proof that the mother was cold or that emotional deprivation definitely occurred. It describes a possible inner image and perceptual pattern.

The constructive expression is emotional mastery through patient self-support rather than emotional denial.
