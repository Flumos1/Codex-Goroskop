## Simple Explanation

Compatibility should be described across several axes instead of as one verdict.

A pair can have easy attraction but difficult communication, strong practical cooperation but emotional friction, or useful growth potential that still needs clear boundaries.

Multi-axis output gives people something they can actually work with rather than a flat yes or no.

Reflection: Which axis is strongest here: ease, friction, emotional needs, communication, practical cooperation, growth, or boundaries?

## Advanced Explanation

The report framework should expose multiple interpretation axes: ease, friction, emotional needs, communication, family and care, practical cooperation, growth, and boundaries.

Each axis should be supported by the relevant source layer and should remain separable in the output so later systems can add Western synastry, personology, Jyotish matching, or Chinese/Eastern compatibility without merging methods.

Do not compress all axes into a single compatibility score unless the score is clearly secondary and explained as a rough interface aid.

The best public output helps users notice what works, what needs care, and where a relationship benefits from explicit agreements.
