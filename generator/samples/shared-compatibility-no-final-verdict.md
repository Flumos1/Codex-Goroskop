## Simple Explanation

The project should avoid saying that a relationship is guaranteed, doomed, perfect, or impossible.

Compatibility language should describe patterns of interaction, not decide what people must do.

This protects the user and keeps the system aligned with reflective astrology rather than relationship determinism.

Reflection: Does this wording help people understand a pattern, or does it pretend to decide the relationship for them?

## Advanced Explanation

Compatibility output must pass a safety filter that removes final verdicts, blame labels, inevitability claims, and deterministic relationship instructions.

When the source uses stronger compatibility framing, transform it into tendency language with uncertainty and practical reflection.

Avoid claims such as perfect match, doomed relationship, bad parent, bad child, must marry, must separate, or guaranteed betrayal.

Use compatibility as a reflective map for attention, communication, care, boundaries, and repair.
