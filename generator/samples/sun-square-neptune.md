# Sample Report: Sun square Neptune

- System: western
- Method family: psychological-archetypal
- Confidence: medium
- Source rule: rule.western.aspect-to-inner-statement
- Source IDs: source.greene-sasportas-development-personality-v1

## Simple Explanation

Your identity may be shaped by sensitivity, imagination, longing, or an ideal that is hard to fully define.

There may be a tendency to idealize, dissolve, sacrifice, or search for something larger than ordinary self-definition.

The growth path is to give imagination and compassion a clear form, so sensitivity becomes vision rather than confusion.

Reflection: Where do you need a clearer boundary between inspiration and self-erasure?

## Advanced Explanation

Sun square Neptune is read as tension between the solar principle of identity, will, and self-expression and the Neptunian principle of dissolution, idealization, sacrifice, and transcendence.

The psychological method may include father-image symbolism, but only as an inner image or expectation, not as a factual biography.

Do not treat this as proof of an absent, weak, or deceptive father. It describes a symbolic filter around identity and the solar image.

The constructive expression is creative vision, spiritual sensitivity, and compassionate selfhood with stronger boundaries.
