## Simple Explanation

A Vedic timing period should be read as a context-rich theme, not as a guaranteed event.

The meaning of a dasha depends on the period planet's house ownership, placement, condition, dispositor, and whether other chart factors repeat the same theme.

This makes timing language more useful and less fatalistic: it can describe what becomes emphasized without pretending to know exactly what must happen.

Reflection: What theme is repeated by the natal chart, the period ruler, and any current transits?

## Advanced Explanation

This rule treats dasha interpretation as a layered assessment: mahadasha, antardasha, house ownership, placement, condition, dispositor, and reinforcing or contradicting transit context.

The generator should not output a dasha claim until it can name the period ruler and the chart logic used to support the timing statement.

Do not use dashas for death prediction, medical certainty, financial certainty, or unavoidable event claims in the public product.

The constructive use is thematic timing awareness: a period may invite attention, preparation, reflection, or disciplined effort around a repeated chart topic.
