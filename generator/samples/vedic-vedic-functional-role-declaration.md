## Simple Explanation

In Jyotish, a planet is not interpreted only by its general symbolic nature.

Its role changes according to the Lagna and the houses it owns. The same graha can act as supportive, challenging, mixed, or context-dependent in different charts.

A careful Vedic report should first identify the reference point and the planet's functional role before turning it into advice or timing language.

Reflection: Which reference point is being used for this statement: Lagna, Moon, Sun, or another special reference?

## Advanced Explanation

This rule follows the Jyotish principle that functional benefic and malefic roles depend on Lagna and house ownership, not only on natural graha status.

Before a graha interpretation is generated, the system should record Lagna, houses owned by the graha, placement, and whether the source framework treats the role as supportive, challenging, mixed, or context-dependent.

Do not equate this with Western planetary dignity or psychological planet meaning. Do not generate a Jyotish planet claim when Lagna and house ownership are unknown.

The useful public form is transparent method language: 'in this Jyotish framework, from this reference point, this graha is being treated as...'
