# Applied Esoteric Knowledge Base

This folder tracks practical symbolic systems that may be used as optional overlay layers.

## Current Source Families

- Mazova practical astrology and esoteric books.
- Semira / Vetash name, color, sound, and symbol books.

## Product Role

This material may support:

- calendar and day-of-week overlays;
- talisman, plant, mineral, name, color, and sound symbolism;
- gentle lifestyle and reflection prompts;
- optional esoteric add-ons for users who explicitly request them.

## Safety

Do not turn talismans, plants, minerals, angels, dreams, or household-spirit material into medical, financial, or guaranteed event claims.

Use this branch as symbolic and reflective, not as proof or certainty.
