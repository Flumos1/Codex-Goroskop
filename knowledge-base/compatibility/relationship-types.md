# Compatibility Relationship Types

Every compatibility report must declare the relationship context before interpretation.

## Core Types

- `romantic`: dating, attraction, lovers, early relationship dynamics.
- `marriage-long-term`: spouse, long-term partner, shared household, long commitments.
- `parent-child`: general parent and child relationship.
- `mother-child`: mother and child relationship.
- `father-child`: father and child relationship.
- `friendship`: friends, close friends, social companions.
- `work-business`: colleagues, employer and employee, business partners.
- `family-social`: siblings, relatives, extended family, group and social ties.

## Output Axes

Compatibility is not a single score. Use several axes:

- ease;
- friction;
- emotional needs;
- communication;
- family and care;
- practical cooperation;
- growth;
- boundaries.

## Source Basis

The first extracted source basis is Goldschneider's `Полный гороскоп совместимости`, which explicitly frames relationship comparison across friend, parent, child, lover, spouse, coworker, employer, and employee contexts.

After the OCR sweep, the broader Goldschneider compatibility set is readable and should be used to refine these contexts:

- love, family, and business relationships;
- karmic/personology path comparison;
- success/work cooperation;
- birthday-personology source-only partner portraits.
