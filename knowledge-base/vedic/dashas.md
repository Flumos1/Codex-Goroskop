# Vedic Dashas

Source basis:

- Narasimha Rao, Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach.

## Definition

Dashas divide life into periods, subperiods, and smaller subperiods.

Terms:

- Mahadasha: major period.
- Antardasha: subperiod.
- Pratyantardasha: sub-subperiod.

## Major Families

| Family | Basis | Project Use |
|---|---|---|
| Nakshatra dasha | Moon's nakshatra or related lunar mansion logic | Timing themes. |
| Rashi dasha | Signs/rashis | Timing themes. |
| Phalita dasha | General results | Use cautiously. |
| Ayur dasha | Longevity/death timing | Exclude from public product. |

## Safety Rule

Dashas can be used for timing themes, but not for guaranteed events, medical diagnosis, or death prediction.
