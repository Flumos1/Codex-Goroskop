# Vedic Rashis

Source basis:

- Narasimha Rao, Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach.

## Definition

The zodiac is divided into 12 rashis of 30 degrees each.

Vedic astrology generally uses the sidereal / nirayana zodiac.
