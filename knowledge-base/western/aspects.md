## Psychological Interpretation Layer

Initial source:

- Greene / Sasportas, Development of Personality, Vol. 1.

In the psychological-archetypal layer, aspects can be interpreted as inner statements or expectations.

| Aspect Type | Psychological Use | Generator Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hard aspects | Tension, conflict, pressure, split needs, repeated expectations | Translate into a growth question and constructive channel. |
| Soft aspects | Flow, supportive pattern, available resource | Present as a resource, not as guaranteed success. |
| Planet combinations | Interaction between archetypal principles | Describe the belief or expectation that may arise from the combination. |

Example:

Moon/Saturn tension can be rendered as a pattern around emotional restraint, unmet needs, seriousness around care, or the need to develop mature emotional self-support.
