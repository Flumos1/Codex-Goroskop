## Interpretation Pipeline

Initial source:

- Ushkova / Zakharova, Houses Of The Horoscope.

Use:

1. House number gives the life area.
2. Sign on cusp gives the style of entering that area.
3. Ruler of the cusp sign shows where the topic is routed.
4. Planets in the house show active forces in that area.
5. Empty houses still matter and are read through cusp and ruler.
