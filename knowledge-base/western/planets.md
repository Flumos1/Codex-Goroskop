## Psychological Parent And Inner Image Layer

Initial source:

- Greene / Sasportas, Development of Personality, Vol. 1.

| Planet | Inner Image Use | Generator Caution |

| Moon | Emotional need, early containment, inner mother-image, memory of care | Frame as perception or expectation, not factual mother description. |
| Sun | Identity, self-expression, inner father-image, life direction | Frame as symbolic father/self-image material, not factual father description. |
| Saturn | Boundary, limit, restraint, maturity, work, crystallization | Avoid fear language; include constructive mastery. |
| Neptune | Idealization, dissolution, sacrifice, longing, spiritualization | Avoid implying confusion or loss is inevitable. |
