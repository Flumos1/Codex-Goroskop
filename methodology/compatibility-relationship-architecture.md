# Compatibility And Relationship Architecture

Compatibility is a product domain, not a single astrological tradition.

It can draw from several branches, but each claim must keep its source system visible:

- Western synastry / composite / psychological relationship astrology.
- Personology and calendar-period systems.
- Vedic matching in a later Jyotish-specific layer.
- Chinese / East Asian compatibility in a later source-backed layer.

## Relationship Types

The compatibility module should support different relationship contexts:

- Romantic partners.
- Marriage / long-term partnership.
- Parent and child.
- Mother and child.
- Father and child.
- Friends.
- Work / business partners.
- Siblings or relatives.
- General interpersonal dynamics.

The same pair can be read differently depending on the relationship type.

## Product Rule

Do not output a generic "compatible / incompatible" verdict.

Instead, split compatibility into:

1. Attraction and ease.
2. Friction and recurring misunderstandings.
3. Emotional needs.
4. Communication pattern.
5. Family / care pattern when relevant.
6. Work or practical cooperation when relevant.
7. Growth task.
8. Boundaries and safety.

## Source Discipline

Every compatibility statement should store:

- Source system.
- Relationship type.
- Input basis: birth charts, dates, periods, zodiac systems, or other source method.
- Confidence.
- Safety caution.

## Safety Boundaries

Avoid:

- "You must be together."
- "This relationship cannot work."
- Deterministic claims about divorce, betrayal, abuse, fertility, or family fate.
- Blame toward parents, children, or partners.

Prefer:

- "This pairing may emphasize..."
- "A recurring pattern to watch..."
- "This relationship benefits from..."
- "The difficult side is easier when..."

## First Source Lane

Gary Goldschneider's compatibility material is useful for an initial relationship taxonomy because it explicitly discusses many relationship types:

- Friend.
- Parent.
- Child.
- Lover.
- Spouse.
- Coworker / employer / employee.

Use it as a source-backed relationship-language lane, but do not treat it as final synastry doctrine.

## Generator Rules To Add Later

### Rule: Relationship Type Declaration

Before compatibility interpretation, state the relationship context.

Example:

- Romantic.
- Parent-child.
- Friends.
- Work partners.

### Rule: Multi-Axis Compatibility

Compatibility should be rendered across several axes rather than one score:

- Ease.
- Tension.
- Emotional climate.
- Communication.
- Practical cooperation.
- Growth.

### Rule: No Verdict Compatibility

Compatibility output should not reduce a relationship to "good" or "bad".

It should show patterns, conditions, and constructive practices.
