# Forecasting Boundaries

## Forecast Definition

In this project, a forecast is a structured symbolic reading of time cycles.

It describes likely themes, pressures, openings, and reflective questions. It does not promise exact events.

## Forecast Levels

### Level 1: Theme

Broad psychological or life-area emphasis.

Example:

- Increased attention to responsibility, structure, or long-term commitments.

### Level 2: Pattern

A likely inner or behavioral pattern.

Example:

- The person may feel a stronger need to define boundaries or make mature decisions.

### Level 3: Timing Window

A period when the theme may be more noticeable.

Example:

- The theme may be active around the exact transit and during nearby weeks.

### Level 4: Reflection

A useful question or practice.

Example:

- Where am I being asked to become more honest about limits?

## Forbidden Forecast Forms

Do not generate:

- Certain events.
- Death predictions.
- Medical diagnoses.
- Claims of unavoidable misfortune.
- Instructions to abandon treatment, work, relationships, or legal responsibilities.

## Confidence Labels

Use confidence labels for advanced mode:

- `low`: weak or isolated factor.
- `medium`: repeated theme across several factors.
- `high`: strong repeated pattern across major chart factors.

Confidence means interpretive coherence, not scientific certainty.
