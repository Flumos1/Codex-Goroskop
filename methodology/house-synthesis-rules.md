# House Synthesis Rules

## Source Basis

Initial source:

- Ushkova / Zakharova, Houses Of The Horoscope.

## Rule 1: House Gives Life Area

The house answers "where in life?".

Planets and signs should not be interpreted without locating the life area in which they operate.

## Rule 2: Cusp-Ruler-Resident Pipeline

For house analysis:

1. House number gives the topic.
2. Sign on cusp gives approach, tone, and entry style.
3. Ruler of the cusp sign shows where the house topic is routed.
4. Planets in the house show active forces inside the life area.
5. Empty houses are still interpreted through cusp and ruler.

## Rule 3: Planets In Houses Are Strongly Visible

Planets in a house can be more immediately visible than the ruler alone.

Generator use:

- If a planet is in a house, include it in the simple report.
- If many planets are in a house, mark the house as emphasized.

## Rule 4: Opposite Houses Form Axes

Each house should be interpreted with awareness of its opposite house:

- 1 / 7: self and other.
- 2 / 8: personal and shared resources.
- 3 / 9: nearby and distant knowledge.
- 4 / 10: roots and public achievement.
- 5 / 11: personal joy and collective future.
- 6 / 12: practical and hidden service.

## Rule 5: Empty Does Not Mean Absent

An empty house does not mean the life area is absent or irrelevant.

Use:

- Cusp sign.
- Ruler.
- Aspects to ruler.
- Transits/progressions in future forecasting.

## Rule 6: Safety By House

Some houses require special caution:

- 2nd and 8th: no financial guarantees.
- 5th: no pregnancy or child fate claims.
- 6th and 12th: no medical diagnosis.
- 7th: no deterministic marriage or divorce claims.
- 8th: no death prediction.
- 10th: no guaranteed status or career success.
