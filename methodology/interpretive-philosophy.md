# Interpretive Philosophy

## Core Position

This project treats astrology as a symbolic interpretive system, not as a mechanism for guaranteed prediction.

The goal is to help a person reflect on life patterns, inner dynamics, timing themes, choices, and spiritual or psychological growth.

## What The Project Offers

- Natal chart interpretation.
- Western and Vedic perspectives.
- Psychological and spiritual reflection.
- Forecast themes based on cycles and timing methods.
- Practical prompts for self-observation.
- Advanced methodological transparency.

## What The Project Avoids

- Entertainment-only horoscope wording.
- Guaranteed future events.
- Fatalistic identity labels.
- Medical diagnosis.
- Financial, legal, or relationship commands.
- Fear-based predictions.

## Language Standard

Prefer:

- "This can indicate..."
- "One possible expression is..."
- "A useful question to explore..."
- "This period may emphasize..."
- "In this tradition, this factor is read as..."

Avoid:

- "You will definitely..."
- "This always means..."
- "You are doomed to..."
- "Do not do this because the chart says so..."

## Human Help Standard

Every generated interpretation should leave the user with:

- More self-understanding.
- Less fatalism.
- A practical reflective step.
- A clear distinction between symbol and certainty.
