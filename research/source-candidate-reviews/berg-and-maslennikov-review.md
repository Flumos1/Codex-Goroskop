## Maslennikov: Theory Of Changes

### Preliminary Value

Useful, but not as a direct astrology textbook.

Likely role:

- Theory of cycles and transformations.
- Structural model of change.
- Support for the project's "future as evolving state" philosophy.
- Possible inspiration for knowledge graph design and transition modeling.
- Comparative symbolic system related to I Ching / Book of Changes.

### Why It Matters For This Project

The project needs more than static meanings like "planet X means Y".

A serious generator should understand:

- States.
- Transitions.
- Cycles.
- Tension between stable and changing forces.
- Sequence of transformation.
- Symbolic structures that can be mapped as graphs.

Maslennikov appears useful for this layer.

### Cautions

- It is not an astrology source.
- It should not overwrite astrological methodology.
- It should be used as a meta-methodological or comparative model.
- Claims connecting ancient and modern knowledge should be handled critically.

### Recommendation

Keep and analyze after the core Western/Jyotish foundation is stable.

Potential category:

- `07-symbolic-systems-and-change-theory`

Priority:

- 2 for methodology/graph design.
- 3 for user-facing astrology interpretation.
