## Project Role

These volumes are useful as the first applied Jyotish expansion after Narasimha Rao.

They are especially useful for:

- Functional benefic / malefic logic by Lagna.
- House lordship and house strength.
- Dasha and antardasha result framing.
- Linking periods with transits.
- Preserving Jyotish as a technical system rather than a renamed Western system.

## Core Interpretive Ideas

### 1. Functional Role Depends On Lagna

A graha is not interpreted only by its natural nature. Its role changes through house ownership for a given Lagna.

Project rule:

- Do not generate a Jyotish planet statement without recording Lagna and functional role.
- A public report may say "in this framework, the planet is treated as supportive/challenging for this chart" only when the calculation layer can support that claim.

### 2. House Lordship Matters Before Isolated Placement

The source repeatedly treats house lords, house strength, dispositors, aspects, and placement as a linked assessment.

Generator use:

- Avoid single-factor claims such as "Mars in X gives Y" when Jyotish context is missing.
- Prefer layered language: graha nature, house ownership, placement, aspects, and strength.

### 3. Dasha Results Are Contextual

Volume 3 emphasizes that periods are interpreted through the planet's house ownership, placement, condition, dispositor, and related factors.

Project safety rule:

- Dasha output should be thematic and contextual.
- Avoid guaranteed event prediction.
- Do not use longevity or death-timing content in public product mode.

### 4. Transit Alone Is Not Enough

Transit indications are treated alongside dasha context, not as isolated certainty.

Project rule:

- Do not produce a firm Jyotish timing claim from transit alone.
- Use "period emphasis" language until a future timing module can combine dasha and transit evidence.

### 5. Medical And Family Topics Need Safety Filters

Volume 2 contains health, family, children, and longevity material.

Project boundary:

- Health material remains research-only unless transformed into non-diagnostic symbolic language.
- Children, marriage, and family topics must avoid certainty, fear, and deterministic claims.

## Generator Rules To Add Later

### Rule: Functional Role Declaration

Before interpreting a Jyotish graha, state:

- Lagna used.
- Houses owned by the graha.
- Whether the source framework treats the graha as functionally supportive, challenging, mixed, or context-dependent.

### Rule: Dasha Context Before Timing

Before interpreting a period:

1. Identify dasha and subperiod.
2. Identify house ownership.
3. Identify placement and condition.
4. Identify whether transits reinforce or contradict the period theme.
5. Render as thematic emphasis, not guaranteed event.

### Rule: Public Safety Filter For Sensitive Bhavas

If a Jyotish rule touches 6th, 8th, 12th, disease, children, marriage, or longevity:

- Use cautious symbolic language.
- Avoid diagnosis, death timing, pregnancy certainty, and marital determinism.
- Prefer reflective phrasing and uncertainty markers.
