## Product Role

This source is treated as the project's main primary-wave source for birthday-based analysis.

Use it for:

- a source-only birthday portrait;
- quick partner / important-person analysis from date of birth;
- the first layer in a stereo report, where later astrology, compatibility, Kabbalah, Jyotish, Chinese / Eastern, or symbolic systems are clearly marked as overlays.

## Methodological Placement

The source belongs near the esoteric / Kabbalistic product branch because the product may present it alongside Kabbalah and symbolic systems.

However, the method should be labeled as birthday personology unless later source review proves a specific Kabbalistic technique.

Project rule:

- Do not silently call this Kabbalah.
- Do not silently merge it with Western natal astrology or Jyotish.
- In reports, declare the mode: `birthday-personology / Goldschneider primary wave`.

## Core Structure To Extract

The OCR profile shows a calendar-by-day structure with zodiacal context.

Reusable extraction targets:

- day-of-year portrait;
- personality emphasis;
- strengths and risks;
- advice / growth prompt;
- zodiac period / cusp context;
- partner-analysis use when only a birth date is known.

## Primary Wave / Overlay Rule

The report should separate layers:

- Primary wave: Goldschneider birthday portrait.
- Overlay wave: project-added source-backed layers, such as compatibility, psychological astrology, Kabbalah, Jyotish, Chinese / Eastern astrology, or symbolic systems.

Public wording pattern:

- "Primary wave: from Goldschneider's birthday-personology source..."
- "Overlay: from the project's broader astrology knowledge base..."
- "If the layers differ, read that as a contrast of symbolic lenses, not as a contradiction that proves one side false."

## Safety

Birthday-personology output must avoid:

- fixed personality labels;
- prophecy claims;
- relationship determinism;
- replacing real knowledge of the person;
- implying that one birth date explains the whole person.

Prefer:

- "this source emphasizes...";
- "this portrait may describe a recurring style...";
- "use this as a first lens, then compare with lived context."
